Workshop on Masonry Vaults and Jack Arch Roofs
This workshop was conducted on November 21 & 29, 2023 and December 08, 2023 under the guidance of Sustainability Contractor Mr. Manoj and the MMBC studio faculty team. Students learnt the historical insights to hands-on construction, they explored traditional and new techniques, fostering teamwork and problem-solving. The outdoor studio fostered their creativity, merging theory with practice.
