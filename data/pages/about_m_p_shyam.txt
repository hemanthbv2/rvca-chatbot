Leadership Message
Dr. M.P. Shyam
President
"Architecture should speak of its time and place, but yearn for timelessness" – Frank Gehry.
For over eighty years, RV Educational Institutions has championed quality education in Karnataka. Embodying "Excellence in Education with Societal Commitment" we embrace all students, especially those differently-abled and economically disadvantaged. Through education, we sow the seeds of wisdom and compassion.
Building upon this legacy, RV College of Architecture (RVCA) has emerged as a beacon of academic prowess, dedicating itself to shaping the next generation of architectural visionaries through rigorous academia and innovative pedagogy.
Over the past two and a half decades, RV College of Architecture (RVCA) has built a reputation for excellence in academics, research and consultancy in Architecture and Urban Design. Recognised globally, the institute is dedicated to fostering academic rigour, self-criticism and the shaping of young minds through diverse faculty expertise. Our faculty's contributions to research and the development of innovative teaching tools ensure the highest standards of education and the advancement of urban design.
Our commitment to education extends far beyond the confines of classroom instruction. We have dedicated ourselves to instilling our heritage's deep-rooted values and cherished traditions, shaping not just students but stewards of our nation's cultural legacy.
