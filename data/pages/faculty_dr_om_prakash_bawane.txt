Dr. Om Prakash Bawane Principal RVCA | Architecture Bangalore
Dr. Om Prakash Bawane
Principal
B.Arch.[NIT, Bhopal], M.Arch.[IIT, Roorkee], M.sc.(Sust.development)[Sikkim Manipal University], Ph.D. [Vinayaka Missions University]
Linkedin [https://www.linkedin.com/in/dr-om-prakash-bawane-1a23b068/]
With three decades of academic and administrative experience, Dr. Bawane is one of the senior professors of architecture in Karnataka. Recipient of the Teachers’ Day Award, our Principal is an alumnus of NIT Bhopal and IIT Roorkee.
He specialises in areas of climatic design, building services and quality management in construction and has visited several architecture schools across the country to deliver talks.
He has also been involved with the Council of Architecture in different capacities and held the position of Chairman, of VTU’s Board of Studies in Architecture.
Specialisation and interest: Sustainable Development, Building Services
