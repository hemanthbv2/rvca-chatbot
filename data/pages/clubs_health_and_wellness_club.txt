RVCA | Focus on The Overall Physical and Mental Health
Clubs
Health & Wellness Club
RVCA Health & Wellness Club aspires to focus on the overall physical and mental health of the students through various programmes, sessions by experts, and healing practices. Aspects such as motivation, time management, good practices of posture, self-care etc will be covered for improving the overall development of students on campus.
