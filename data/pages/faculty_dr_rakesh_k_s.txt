Dr. Rakesh K S B.Arch & M.Arch | Architecture Bangalore
Dr. Rakesh K. S.
Principal
B. Arch. [SAP, Chennai], M. Arch. [SAP, Chennai], Ph.D. [SAP, Chennai]
Linkedin [https://www.linkedin.com/in/rakesh-k-s/]
Dr. Rakesh K. S. received his B. Arch., M. Arch., and Ph.D. degrees from the School of Architecture and Planning [SAP], Anna University, Chennai.
He has been an academician for more than 27 years, holding administrative positions and teaching in various institutions like SPA Vijayawada, Sathyabama University Chennai, RVS Institutions Coimbatore, Nehru Institutions Palakkad and BMS School of Architecture Bengaluru.
His interests span climatic design, sustainable architecture, and contemporary architecture.
He has been a member of the Board of Studies [B.Arch. & M.Arch.] at SPA Vijayawada, Anna University Chennai, Sathyabama University Chennai, and the University of Calicut, Kerala.
He has been associated with the Council of Architecture as an Inspection Committee member. He has served as the Treasurer of the IIA Palakkad Chapter and as the Co-Convener of the INTACH Palakkad Chapter.
He served as a member of the Committee for monitoring and advising on new campus construction at IIT Palakkad.
He has been invited for guest lectures at various institutions including IIT Palakkad, XIM University Bhubaneshwar, and the School of Architecture and Planning, Chennai. He has served as an External Examiner at various institutions.
He has served as a Doctoral Committee member at Sathyabama University Chennai, Ph.D. Examiner at Sushant University, Gurugram and at various institutions under Visvesvaraya Technological University.
He is a registered doctoral supervisor in the Faculty of Architecture at Visvesvaraya Technological University.
He is a reviewer of two Scopus Indexed Journals – Journal of the Institution of Engineers (India) and the Indian Journal of Traditional Knowledge (IJTK) published by CSIR.
Specialisation and interest: Climatic Design, Sustainable Architecture
