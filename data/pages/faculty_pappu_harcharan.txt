Mr. Harcharan Pappu holds a Bachelor’s degree in Architecture (B.Arch) from the School of Planning and Architecture, Vijayawada (2014) and a Master in Urban and Regional Planning from the University of Mysore (2017). He has nearly 5 years of experience in both practice and academia with a keen interest towards research. He has published a research paper in an international Journal and also presented a technical paper at a National conference for which he was awarded a gold medal.
Specialisation and interest: Urban and Regional Planning
