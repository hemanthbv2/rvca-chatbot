TITLE: K Sreedhar Professor RVCA Architecture College Bangalore

K Sreedhar Professor RVCA Architecture College Bangalore
K Sreedhar
Professor, Social Welfare Officer
B.Arch.[JNTU, Andhra Pradesh], M. Design[IIT-Bombay]
Linkedin [http://www.linkedin.com/in/k-sreedhar-2b9836318]
Prof. Sreedhar K holds a Bachelor of Architecture degree from JNT University, Hyderabad (1983) and specialised as a product designer by doing a Master of Design, Graduate course from IIT, Bombay (1985). He is one of the senior faculty of this institute, teaching here since 2008.
He has teaching experience of 33 years (since 1987), teaching architectural subjects in various institutions in Karnataka. His expertise is in teaching subjects such as Architectural Graphics, Freehand and presentation drawings, the History of Indian and colonial architecture, and Architectural and Design Model making techniques.
He has conducted architectural study tours for the students and held workshops on architectural presentations and creative design skills.
He was involved in many guest lectures as visiting faculty at other institutions. His interests are to study and document the Heritage Buildings, and Hindu temples of Karnataka.
Specialisation and interest:  Architecture & Product Design
