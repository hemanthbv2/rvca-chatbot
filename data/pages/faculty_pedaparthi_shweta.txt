Shweta Pedaparthi is an architect, educator, and practitioner whose work reflects a strong engagement with both academic and professional realms of architecture. She completed her Bachelor of Architecture from RV College of Architecture, Bengaluru, and later pursued a Master of Business Administration from Jain University, adding a managerial and strategic dimension to her architectural training.
Early in her professional career, she worked with Praveen Vashisht and Associates in Delhi, where she gained exposure to diverse architectural practices and project typologies. She subsequently joined RSP Design Consultants, Bengaluru, one of the leading international design firms, where she contributed to a wide range of projects. This experience enabled her to develop a comprehensive understanding of design processes, project coordination, and professional practice.
Driven by a strong inclination towards teaching, Ar. Shweta transitioned into academia and served as an Assistant Professor at R.R. School of Architecture, Bengaluru. During her tenure, she engaged with undergraduate students across multiple subjects, including Architectural Design, Building Construction and Materials, Theory of Architecture, and Climatology. Her teaching approach emphasizes clarity, contextual understanding, and the integration of theory with practice.
Since joining RV College of Architecture in 2017, she has played a significant role in both academic and co-curricular domains. Beyond her teaching responsibilities, she actively contributes to student engagement through cultural activities and alumni initiatives, fostering a holistic learning environment. Her dual role as an educator and practicing architect allows her to bring real-world insights into the classroom.
In her professional practice, she demonstrates a keen interest in exploring pragmatic and efficient approaches to space planning and management. Her work reflects a careful attention to functionality, user experience, and contextual responsiveness.
Ar. Shweta has also been instrumental in initiating and supporting new academic and institutional initiatives within the college, contributing to the continuous development of teaching methodologies and student engagement platforms. Through her combined efforts in teaching, practice, and institutional development, she continues to contribute meaningfully to architectural education and the shaping of future professionals.
