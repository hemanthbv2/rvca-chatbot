Quiz Club
The Quiz Club aims to motivate, inspire, encourage, and reward students in their pursuit of knowledge. It provides an opportunity for students to celebrate their achievements, develop general knowledge, stay updated on current affairs, and engage in a healthy competitive environment. The club's objectives are to foster a love of learning and promote intellectual growth among students.
