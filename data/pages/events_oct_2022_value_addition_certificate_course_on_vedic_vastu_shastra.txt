Value addition certificate course on “Vedic Vastu Shastra”
The objective of this intense course conducted between the months of October 19 to November 26, 2022 was to educate students on traditional Vedic Vaastu principles and adaption in contemporary perspective. The programme was curated by RVCA faculty along with the guidance of invited expert Ch. Bhaskar, Vaastu Consultant.
