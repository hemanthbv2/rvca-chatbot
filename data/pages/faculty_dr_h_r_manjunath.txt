Dr H R Manjunath is a Civil Engineer and a Structural Engineer with a Doctorate. He has more than 30 years of teaching and research experience in the engineering field. He worked in well-established colleges like KVG College of Engineering, Sullia and M S Ramaiah Institute of Technology, Bangalore before joining RV College of Architecture in the year 2011. He has guided several undergraduate, post graduate students and presently guiding research scholars. Presently he is working as an Associate Professor and Associate Dean for VTU affairs. He has published several technical papers in national and international journals and conferences. He is actively involved in the board of studies/examinations of VTU and other autonomous institutions. As a researcher, his field of interest is in concrete materials and related structures.
Specialisation and interest: Civil Engineering & Structural Engg.
