Achievements/Scholarship
The student of RV College of architecture Vanessa TRESSA D’souza represented INDIA, participated in GACC International inter-varsity Chess Championship held at Tuanku Bahiyah school, University of Malaysia, and secured 2 nd Position.
The student of RV College of architecture Anupozu khyathi represented INDIA, in 18 th Asian Senior Rollers Skating Championship held at south Korea, bagged silver medal.  She also  participated in other  international and national level skating tournaments and won two medals and trophies.
Mohammad Mir Abies Raza , Represented Karnataka Football Association, The Swami Vivekananda U-20 men’s National Football Championship-2024 tournament organized by Indian Football Association this tournament held in Chhattisgarh from 12 th April 2024 to 25 th may 2024.
Rakshit.V Participated in VTU State level athletic meet-2024-25, 100-meter race along with the 120 participants from various colleges, He won Silver Medal for the college.
The student of RV college of architecture Anu Dhananjay represented Karnataka zone women's cricket team – 2025, event organized by BCCI (Board of Control for cricket in India)
