Rendering Remedies - A value addition certificate course
RVCA organised a value addition certificate course to help the students enhance their visual representation skills that will be useful for application in various subjects, and architecture as a whole. It helped them refine and further develop their presentation and representation skills in their current and upcoming semester Architectural Design and Basic Design portfolio.
This was a wholesome attempt to enhance presentation skills and make drawings and sheets more presentable.
Number of Participants: 24
Faculty Incharge: Ar. Alisha Sinha & Ar. Akanksha Singh
