SITE VISITS
Site Visits offer practical architecture experience outside the classroom. Explore real buildings, meet experts, and learn from design solutions.
Bridge the gap between theory and practise together! Site Visits offer practical experience in architecture outside the classroom. Explore completed buildings, meet experts, observe design solutions, and engage in discussions. These visits help bridge theoretical knowledge and real-world applications. Participants gain essential skills, a competitive edge, and a passion for continuous learning.
