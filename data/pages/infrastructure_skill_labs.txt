RVCA | Skills Labs Enrich With NewTechnology
Experiential Learning
SKILL LABS
Our design studios and labs are a valuable resource for designers of all levels. It offers a collaborative and innovative environment with ample workspace and prototyping tools. Whether you are a student, a professional designer, or an entrepreneur, our facilities can provide you with the tools and resources you need to bring your design ideas to life.
Clay/ Pottery Station
Clay Studio at RVCA stands to foster artistic expression. Clay Studio provides a dynamic space where students, faculty, and artists alike can explore the versatile medium of clay.
With state-of-the-art pottery wheels and kilns, individuals can work on their creativity and transform their ideas into tangible works of art."
Climate Responsive
The lab inculcates an objective and practical approach to evaluate the implication of climate on the built form. The updated equipment and relevant software make the lab very crucial in generating designs sensitive to climate.
Fabrication Lab
The laboratories and equipment support both faculty research and research-based studios and seminars. The lab is a facility for the construction of full-scale building prototypes and mock-ups.
Graphic Analytical and Spatial Analytical Lab
The college has high-tech computer graphics studios with advanced computers and internet services through wireless connectivity, LCD projection systems, hardware and advanced updated software.
GIS Lab
The GIS (Geographic Information System) Lab is a dedicated space equipped with specialised software and hardware to support the analysis, visualisation, and management of geographic data in the RVCA campus.
Model Making Workshop
The lab encourages students to explore different materials like paper, wood, wires, thermocol etc. in model making.
Structural Testing Lab
The lab is to impart practical learning to the students for structural materials like bricks, concrete, timber, steel etc., under different loading conditions as well as to test the quality and properties of the building unit.
