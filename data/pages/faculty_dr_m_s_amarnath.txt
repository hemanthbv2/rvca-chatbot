Dr M S Amarnath RVCA Architecture College Bangalore
Dr M S Amarnath
Associate Professor
B.F.A (Bangalore University), MFA [MSU, Baroda], Ph.D. [Jain University]
Linkedin [https://www.linkedin.com/in/m-s-amarnath-844a621b1/]
Dr. M S Amarnath completed his BFA (Painting) College of Fine Arts, (BU, 1990) and MFA (Painting) M.S.U, Baroda in 1992. He received his PhD in Jain University, Bangalore in 2014. He is working as an Associate Professor in RVCA, Bangalore with an overall teaching experience of 23 years. He teaches Basic Architectural Design, Visual arts, Model making, Product Design, Art in Architecture, Visual communication, art and craft, and Architectural presentation skills. He participated in the International SAARC Artist Camp in Sri Lanka in 2018. He has also participated in the National Painters camp organised By J&K Tourism Development Corporation at Jammu in 1988 & 2004; in the Artist Camp organised by Karnataka State Lalitkala Academy, in Feb 2016 and the Group Art exhibition at Bharathiya Vidyabhavan, London in 2015. He is a recipient of the Karnataka State Lalit Kala Academy Award in 1986, the Karnataka State Shilpa Kala Academy Award in 2000, the INLAKS Scholarship from London 1993 to work in India and the Mysore Dasara Award 1987.
Specialisation and interest:  Fine Arts
