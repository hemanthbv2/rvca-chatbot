Deepthi V
Assistant Professor
Deepthi Varghese is a conservation architect who has completed her B.Arch from SPA Vijayawada and M.Arch (Conservation and Regeneration) from CEPT University, Ahmedabad. With over seven years of experience, she has handled a range of professional roles in reputed institutions in industry as well as in academics. Her teaching experience spans undergraduate courses/studios and postgraduate courses/studios in Conservation and Urban Design. Her ongoing PhD at SPA, Vijayawada focuses on the conservation of modern heritage in India. She has co-edited two books and organized multiple national/international workshops apart from a Capacity Building Programme under A-CUPCB at SPAV.
Her other areas of interest are architectural documentation, participatory design and statistical data analysis.
