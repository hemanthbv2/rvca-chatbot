Trail - Travel and Photography Club
Trail is RVCA's travel and photography club. Here, students explore places and things, taking pictures and making memories. They use cameras to capture moments and create awesome reels. From quiet nature spots to busy cities, Trail helps students learn about different places and improve their photo skills. It's a fun club where everyone shares a love for travel and telling stories through pictures.
