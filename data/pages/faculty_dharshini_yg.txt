Dharshini YG Architecture College Bangalore
Dharshini YG
Senior Assistant Professor
B.Arch.[VTU], M.Arch.(Urban Design)[VTU]
Linkedin [https://www.linkedin.com/in/prakruthi-s-karadagi/]
Dharshini is a distinguished professional who graduated with a Bachelor's degree in Architecture from BVB College of Engineering and Technology, Hubli, and subsequently earned a Master's Degree in Urban Design from R.V. School of Architecture, Bangalore. With over 12 years of comprehensive experience in the field of Architecture, Dharshini has exhibited exceptional expertise.
Before her current role at the esteemed RSP Design Consultants (India) Pvt. Ltd., she contributed her architectural prowess to renowned multi-disciplinary firms and developers such as Expat Projects & Development Pvt. Ltd. Her professional portfolio boasts a diverse range of projects, including the design of IT campuses, mixed-use developments, and commercial and residential projects.
Dharshini embarked on her academic journey in 2022 at RNS School of Architecture, where she delved into Architectural Design, Thesis work, and Building services. This educational foundation, coupled with her extensive professional background, positions her as a seasoned architect capable of integrating theoretical knowledge and practical skills comprehensively. Dharshini's commitment to excellence and her ability to navigate diverse architectural projects make her an invaluable asset in the field.
Specialisation and interest: Urban Design
