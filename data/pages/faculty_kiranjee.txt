Kiranjee - rvcanew
Kiranjee
B. Arch: Visvesvaraya National Institute of Technology (VNIT), Nagpur – 2019
M. Arch in Sustainable Architecture: School of Planning and Architecture (SPA), Vijayawada – 2022
Ar. Kiranjee specializes in Sustainable Architecture and has over four years of experience working on national and city-level programmes focused on climate-responsive and energy-efficient design across building, neighbourhood, and city scales. He is a GRIHA Certified Professional, well-versed with key national and international green building codes and rating systems.
He has contributed to master plan and area development plans for Visakhapatnam, while working with Greater Visakhapatnam Municipal Corporation (GVMC). At Environmental Design Solutions (EDS), he has worked on initiatives focusing on Net-Zero development, decarbonization, and sustainable development goals, in collaboration with public-sector organizations, NGOs, and development partners.
He has also taught at NIT Calicut, handling Sustainable Architecture, HVAC, and Building Science Laboratory courses. His academic approach emphasizes climate-responsive and performance-based architectural design.
