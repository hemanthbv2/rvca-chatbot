Faculty
Richa Bihari
Assistant Professor
B.Arch – Veer Surendra Sai University of Technology, Burla
M.Arch in History and Theory – CEPT University, Ahmedabad
Linkedin [http://www.linkedin.com/in/richabihari]
Richa Bihari is an architect and academic with a keen interest in architectural history,and research. She holds a Master’s degree in Architectural History and Theory from CEPT University, Ahmedabad, and a Bachelor’s degree in Architecture from VSSUT, Burla. With two years of experience as a Teaching Associate at CEPT University, she has guided students in master's housing design and history studios and bachelor history courses, helping them develop their skills and perspectives. Additionally, in her role as a Research Assistant at the Center for Heritage Conservation, she worked on the project 'Art and Architecture of Vadnagar' in collaboration with the Ministry of Culture and Directorate of Archaeology & Museums, Government of Gujarat.
Specialisation and interest: History and Theory, Editorial design and curation
Publications/career highlights
Worked on the 'Art and Architecture of Vadnagar' project at the Center for Heritage Conservation, CEPT Research and Development Foundation, responsible for documentation, research, writing, book design, and compilation. The final output includes a book and two cross-sections of the town, which are yet to be published. This project was carried out in collaboration with the Directorate of Archaeology and Museums, Government of Gujarat, and the Ministry of Culture and Sports, Government of India.
