Aims to Foster an Appreciation For Cultural Diversity
Clubs
Heritage Club
The Heritage Club in our college aims to foster an appreciation for cultural diversity and historical richness. Through a variety of engaging activities, the club strives to promote the preservation of traditional values and customs while encouraging dialogue about heritage in a modern context. Activities include cultural exhibitions, heritage walks, and interactive workshops that explore art, cuisine, and customs from different eras and regions. By creating an inclusive space for students to explore and celebrate their heritage, the club seeks to instil a sense of pride and understanding, fostering a community that values and preserves its cultural legacy for future generations.
