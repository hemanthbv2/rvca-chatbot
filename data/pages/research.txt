SMART CITY RESEARCH AND CONSULTANCY
The Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoHUA) and the National Institute of Urban Affairs (NIUA) jointly initiated SAAR(Smart Cities and Academia Towards Action and Research)a process involving premier academic institutions across India in the documentation of the outcome and learnings from the smart city projects in India. SAAR 1.0 allowed the students to engage with the urban development works and the development process at various scales and conditions. Documentation and sharing of the outcome of the smart city projects strengthened the alliance between academia and urban practice.
SAAR 2.0 enables opportunities for smart city projects to engage with eligible institutions of academic excellence to take advantage of academic approaches that formulate urban programmes as well as academic research.
The on-ground study approach would provide insight into the opinions and positive responses made by the smart city project on the social and economic front. SAAR 2.0 intends to bring academia and urban practices closer together and highlights the importance of collaboration to make any urban project inclusive and resilient.
RVCA is one of the top 15 premier institutes of academic excellence recognised by the Ministry of Housing and Urban Affairs (MoUHA) and the National Institute of Urban Affairs (NIUA). Under the SAAR 1.0 initiative, documented the selected urban projects in Bengaluru, Davanagere and Belagavi smart city.
UDRCC (Urban Design Research and Consultancy Cell) RVCA is currently an ongoing research consultancy project under SAAR 2.0 engaged with Shivamogga and Bengaluru Smart City Limited. This provides opportunities for the students of the Master's Programme to engage in research and consultancy projects.
