PROACTIS
AWARD
The presentation & distribution of The Proactis Awards are being sponsored by M/s. Teekay Interior Solutions Pvt., Ltd., Bengaluru for the past 10 years to the students securing the highest marks(internal) in architectural design of their respective semester. The award carries a cash prize of Rs. 5000/- and a certificate of appreciation. In 2023, the awards were presented by Mr. Umar Teekay, Chairman & Managing Director, M/s. Teekay Interior Solutions Pvt., Ltd., Bengaluru in the presence of Chief Guest Ar. Bijoy Ramachandra, Partner, M/s. Hundredhands & Shri. P.S. Nandakumar, Chairman, Governing Council of RVCA on November 29, 2023.

RATHI
AWARD
Instituted by M/s. Rathi Associates, Bengaluru w.e.f. 2021-22 for 10 years in fond memory of Late Architect Shri. B.H. Rathi. (INR 50K+Medal+Citation) to identify, acknowledge and encourage the most promising budding architects of the country who have represented their innovative ideas, and thinking process through their thesis representation at B. Arch. level.
Ms. Dia Vohra (1RW17AT072), won the second "Ar. B.H. Rathi Best Thesis Award 2023" with winning trophy, medal & cash prize of Rs. 50,000/- for her thesis titled “Uggaha | triggers of change” at the award presentation ceremony held on August 12, 2023.
Mr. Rakshith H.N. (1RW17AT072), won the first “Ar. B.H. Rathi Best Thesis Award 2022” with winning trophy, medal & cash prize of Rs. 50,000/- for his thesis titled “Uggaha | triggers of change” on June 23, 2022.
