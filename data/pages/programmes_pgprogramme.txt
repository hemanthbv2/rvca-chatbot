PG Programme
Cultivating the architects of tomorrow
Take your career in architecture to the next level. Refine your skills, deepen your knowledge, and propel your architectural career forward with RVCA’s postgraduate programmes.
In a rapidly urbanising world, the need for visionary urban designers has never been greater. Dive into a 2-year dynamic curriculum that merges art, science, technology and tradition to craft urban spaces that are sustainable, innovative, and, above all, truly human.
Master of Architecture (M.Arch.)
Indian cities are undergoing rapid growth and transformation, posing new challenges and opportunities for urbanists in reading and theorising the city. We at RVCA are constantly seeking new approaches to engage with the discipline of Urban Design. The classrooms and studios provide vibrant discursive spaces for faculty and students to debate and manifest varied visions for livable and inclusive Indian cities.
COURSE OBJECTIVE
Introducing multidimensional aspects of urbanism to facilitate nuanced readings and interpretations of the urban milieu in India.
Supporting research and instilling academic rigour in the study, critique and design of urban environs.
Promoting a hands-on approach to understanding and engaging with urban issues.
Mentoring  students to become skilled, conscientious and ethical professionals.
COURSE OUTCOME
Able to conduct urban study, research and design independently of urban projects.
Able to handle complex urban challenges effectively and ethically.
