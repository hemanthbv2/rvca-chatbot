COA & VTU Approved Curriculum

Academic Degree Programmes

RVCA provides accredited undergraduate, postgraduate, and doctoral degrees combining deep spatial design philosophy with hands-on studio technology.

Undergraduate
📐

Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch)

5-Year (10 Semesters) flagship degree emphasizing architectural design studios, digital fabrication, building construction technologies, and a mandatory 1-semester professional internship in active architectural practice.

  • Duration: 5 Years (10 Semesters), Full-Time
  • Affiliation: VTU Belagavi & COA New Delhi
  • Intake Routes: KEA KCET (Code E-244), COMEDK, Management Quota
  • Eligibility: 10+2 PCM (min 45%) + NATA or JEE Main Paper II
Postgraduate
🏙️

M.Arch in Urban Design

2-Year (4 Semesters) postgraduate specialization examining spatial morphology, inclusive Indian urban habitats, GIS analytics, and resilient ecological transformations.

  • Duration: 2 Years (4 Semesters), Full-Time
  • Total Seats: 20 Seats (16 PGCET + 4 Management)
  • Eligibility: B.Arch degree with min 50% aggregate
  • Coordinator: Prof. Prasanna Rao (Assoc. Dean PG)
Doctoral Research
🔬

Ph.D. in Architecture

VTU Recognised Research Centre with dedicated faculty doctoral guides investigating sustainable architecture, climatic responsive design, and urban settlements.

  • Centre: VTU Recognised Research Centre
  • Supervisors: Dr. O.P. Bawane, Dr. Shikha Verma, Dr. Mahalakshmi
  • Entrance: VTU-ETR followed by Research Proposal
Zero Broker Direct Applications

Transparent Admissions & Seat Quotas

Admissions at RV College of Architecture are strictly merit-driven across official government and institutional routes. RVCA operates with NO agents, brokers, or consultants.

🏛️

Karnataka Domicile (KEA KCET)

Karnataka state merit counselling conducted online through the Karnataka Examinations Authority (KEA). Rank calculated 50% from 10+2 PCM + 50% from NATA score.

  • KEA College Code: E-244
  • Entrance: NATA (COA) or JEE Main Paper II
  • Official Portal: cetonline.karnataka.gov.in
🇮🇳

All-India Merit (COMEDK UGET)

Centralized online merit counselling for students nationwide based on 10+2 marks and normalized NATA score.

  • Seat Type: All-India Open Merit
  • Eligibility: Min 45% aggregate in 10+2 with PCM
  • Official Portal: comedk.org
🤝

Direct Management Quota (RSST)

Direct institutional merit applications processed by Rashtreeya Sikshana Samithi Trust. Candidates must email marksheets and NATA scorecards directly to 'The Hon. Secretary, RSST'.

  • Criteria: Min 70% in 10th Math/Sci; min 65% in Class 11 PCM
  • Official Email: rv@rvei.edu.in
  • Policy: Strict Zero-Broker Policy
Hands-On Prototyping

Experiential Design Studios & Specialized Labs

Architecture requires tactile exploration. Our purpose-built standalone campus houses advanced prototyping studios and digital fabrication technologies.

📐
Daylit Design Studios

40-student studios with individual drafting tables, lockers, natural daylight, and jury pin-up boards.

🏺
Clay & Pottery Studio

Equipped with motorized pottery wheels and ceramic electric kilns for tactile 3D exploration.

🪵
Model Making Workshop

Precision wood, acrylic, wire, foam, and paper craft modeling tools for architectural mockups.

🖨️
Digital Fabrication Lab

Advanced 3D printers, laser prototyping, and full-scale mock-up fabrication.

☀️
Climate Responsive Lab

Climatological simulation tools to test passive shading, acoustics, and daylight dynamics.

🗺️
GIS & Graphics Studio

High-end workstations with AutoCAD, Revit BIM, SketchUp, Rhino, and ArcGIS mapping.

📚
Comprehensive Library & JSTOR

Over 81 print/e-journals, 2,065 JSTOR journals, Web-OPAC, and a 180 sq. m Material Museum.

🏗️
Structural Testing Lab

Hands-on material testing for masonry, timber, concrete, and steel under structural loads.
