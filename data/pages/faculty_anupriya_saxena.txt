Anupriya Saxena
Associate Professor

Linkedin [https://www.linkedin.com/in/anupriya-saxena-a8468715]
Ms. Anupriya Saxena earned her master’s degree in Building Engineering and Management from the School of Planning and Architecture, New Delhi and was a rank holder in her B. Arch degree from Birla Institute of Technology, Mesra, Ranchi. Being inclined towards Sustainable/environment-responsive architecture, she is an IGBC (Indian Green Building Council) Accredited Professional and also held the post of president for the Environmental Club at BIT. With over 3 years of experience designing and coordinating commercial and institutional building projects in the industry with L&T Construction as Sr. Architect (Asst. Manager), she joined RVCA as an Assistant Professor in 2015 and has been teaching subjects related to Design, Construction, Building Services and Sustainable Architecture, which are her strong suits. She is presently pursuing her PhD from NIT, Tiruchirappalli and her research is related to Circularity in Built Environment
Specialisation and interest: Building Engineering & Management, Climatic Design, Sustainable Architecture, Circularity in Built Environment
