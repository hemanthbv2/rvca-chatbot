RVCA | Creativity Analytical and Critical Skills
Clubs
Book Club/Literary Club
The reading club aims to provide a platform for students to discuss and reflect on what they read. The objectives are to promote reading, present book reviews regularly, and enhance students' creativity, analytical, and critical skills. Intended activities include choosing a specific book to read and discuss simultaneously and discussing books based on a topic or an agreed-upon reading list.
