TITLE: Mayank Singh Architecture College Bangalore

Mayank Singh Architecture College Bangalore
Mayank Singh
Senior Assistant Professor
B.Arch.[BIT, Mesra], M.Tech.(Structural Engg. Design)[CEPT, Ahmedabad]
Linkedin [https://www.linkedin.com/in/mayank-singh-7aa38698/]
Mr. Mayank is an architect with 1.5 years of Architectural practice and a Master’s Specialisation in Structural Engineering with 7 years of Structural design practice under his belt. His main subjects are building construction, with an emphasis on computer technology applications large-span and high-rise structures, and buildings with special geometry like shells and folded plates. He is also a part-time freelance structural design consultant involved in various national and international projects of small to medium scale. As a part-time musician/producer, he is also working to draw parallels with music and architecture for students to develop a cohesive understanding and commonalities of both the disciplines.
Specialisation and interest: Structural Engineering Design, Construction, Engineering, Technology, Computational Design
