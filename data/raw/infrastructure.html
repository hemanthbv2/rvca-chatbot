Experience Learning

Beyond the Classroom

Beyond the physical infrastructure, RVCA offers a vibrant campus life that fosters a sense of community.  Spend time socialising with classmates in our open spaces, participate in extracurricular activities, or network with industry professionals at our events. We invite you to explore our exceptional infrastructure and embark on a transformative journey at RVCA.

Explore a Wealth of Resources at RVCA

RVCA recognises the importance of providing students with extensive resources to support their academic journey and professional aspirations. Thus providing a gateway to a comprehensive collection of valuable assets that can empower your design exploration and fuel your creativity. This is just a glimpse of the extensive resources available at RVCA. We encourage you to actively explore and discover all that we offer.

Library

The RV College of Architecture library is a comprehensive resource centre for students and faculty, supporting a wide range of academic activities with well-stocked collections.

Know More

Multipurpose and Seminar Hall

The college boasts a state-of-the-art seminar hall of 130 capacity, designed for hosting workshops, guest lectures, invited talks, conferences, and presentations.

Know More

Experiential Learning Spaces

The campus houses spacious studios to accommodate a capacity of 40 students each. The studio houses individual work desks & drafting boards.

Know More

Hostel

The hostel facilities at RVCA include forty-three-bed rooms in both the Girls’ and Boys’ hostel blocks.

Know More

Physical Education

Outdoor and indoor sports facilities comprising chess, carrom board, table tennis, basketball, badminton court and gymnasium are available within the premises.

Know More

Canteen

The RVCA canteen serves vegetarian meals from 9:00 AM to 5:00 PM. The mess menu is curated by the Student Committee, offering a variety of delicacies.

Know More

Social Outreach

RVCA’s NSS and Rotaract programmes promote community service, foster leadership, and enhance students’ social responsibility.

Know More

On-Campus Doctor and Counselling Cell

We provide on-campus doctor facilities to ensure the health and well-being of our students.
