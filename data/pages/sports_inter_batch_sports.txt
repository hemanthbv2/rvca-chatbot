Sports
Achievements/Scholarship
Vison of RVCA Sports Fest is “Where athletes and sports enthusiasts come together to compete, cheer, and celebrate the spirit of sportsmanship!”
We have been Organizing annual sports fest every year as RVCA Sports Fest this most awaited event of the year architectural students around in which 400 to 500 participate from various colleges in Karnataka. Conducted 12 Games for Architectural students to motivate and promote sports in RV College of architecture, Bengaluru.
