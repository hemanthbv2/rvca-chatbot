PhD in Architecture at RVCA | Academic & Research Opportunities
Ph.D. programme
Study with us!
Are you a passionate researcher with a burning desire to push the boundaries of architecture? RVCA invites you to join our prestigious Ph.D. programme in Architecture. Designed for highly motivated individuals, our programme will equip you with the advanced research skills and knowledge to become a leading scholar in the field.
Ph.D. Programme
Ph.D. in Architecture program at RVCA is affiliated with Visvesvaraya Technological University. It intends to cultivate advanced research and scholarship in the field of architecture and allied areas. This programme is tailored for individuals with a strong academic background and a passion for conducting original research in architectural theory, history, design, technology, or related areas. The course approach emphasises rigorous academic inquiry, critical thinking, and scholarly writing, culminating in a doctoral dissertation that contributes new knowledge to the field. Scholars under this programme will be well-equipped to pursue careers in academia, research institutions, architectural firms, government agencies etc.
