RVCA | To Ensure the Convenience of the Students
Experiential Learning
Hostel
The hostel facilities at RVCA include forty-three-bed rooms in both the Girls' and Boys' hostel blocks. Additionally, there is a Venkatadri Hostel located about 4 kilometres from the campus, which offers an extra thirty-bed capacity. To ensure the convenience of the students, a vegetarian mess is attached to the hostel, providing four meals a day.
