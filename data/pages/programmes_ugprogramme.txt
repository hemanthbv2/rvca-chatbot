UG Programme
Design Your Future
Evolve from a student to a prodigy in a 5-year programme. With research, planning, presentation, and technical and managerial applications, the programme enables aspirants to be industry-ready and transform spaces as examples of architectural excellence.
Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch.)
We believe architecture is more than just designing buildings. It's about creating spaces that inspire, nurture, and improve our lives. The Bachelor of Architecture programme spans five years (10 semesters). It is affiliated with the Visvesvaraya Technological University, which sets forth the course curriculum. Recognised by the Council of Architecture, the best standards and practices of architectural pedagogy are upheld. Being a professional course, that is inclusive of a semester's internship, it would equip the student with all the relevant knowledge, skills and acumen to set out into Architectural Practice as a competent professional.
COURSE OBJECTIVE
The programme is designed to bridge the gap between academics and practice by inculcating a comprehensive understanding of the environment and ecosystem, the latest knowledge systems, and contemporary practices in design technology and sustainability.
It also emphasises heritage conservation through effective pedagogical methods and training processes, ensuring that students are well-prepared for real-world architectural challenges.
With a profound understanding of architectural principles, theories, and history, design and problem-solving are seamlessly integrated. The ability to skillfully analyse architectural challenges enables the identification of critical issues and promotes the development of novel solutions that resonate with the project's context. This approach transforms abstract concepts into tangible architectural solutions that harmoniously encompass creativity, functionality, and user requirements.
Design eco-conscious architecture committed to sustainability. Uphold ethical conduct in architectural practice, prioritising integrity and society's well-being. Promote collaboration to achieve outstanding architectural results.
Architects efficiently communicate concepts using oral presentations, written reports, and visuals, ensuring comprehension. They possess project management and financial expertise to deliver successful projects within resource constraints. Moreover, they commit to continuous professional development, adapting to changing industry requirements.
