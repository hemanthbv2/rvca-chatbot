Ramya Krishna

Linkedin [https://www.linkedin.com/in/ramya-krishna304/]
Ms. Ramya Krishna earned her undergraduate Bachelor’s Degree in Architecture from BMS College of Engineering, Bangalore ( 2007-2021) and her Master’s degree in Urban Design from the University of Colorado, U.S.A (2016-2018). Her work experience spanning across 6 years includes residential, commercial, hospitality and interior design projects. She has also worked as an Urban Design research assistant (2017-2018) for the Colorado Center for Community Development in the U.S.A. Her main interests include active participation in sustainable building strategies, community development and energy-efficient construction. Whenever the opportunity arises, she actively takes part in animal welfare services.
Specialisation and interest: Urban Design, Community Development, Architecture, Urban Realm, Interior Design
