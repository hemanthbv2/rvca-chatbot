Experiential Learning
Multipurpose and Seminar Hall
The college boasts a state-of-the-art seminar hall of 130 capacity, designed for hosting workshops, guest lectures, invited talks, conferences, and presentations. The multipurpose hall has a seating capacity of about 250 and is equipped with modern amenities such as LCD projectors, screens, whiteboards, and an advanced audio-visual system.
Additionally, the Open Air Amphitheater having 250-capacity, located in the admin building serves as a versatile venue for on-stage events, cultural activities, and music and dance performances. It provides students with a platform to showcase their talents and engage in various artistic and cultural expressions.
