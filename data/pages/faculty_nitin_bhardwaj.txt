Nitin Bhardwaj is an urban designer with a background in architecture and a research focus on urban morphology, participatory urbanism, and sustainable urban practices.
He holds a Diploma in Architecture from Aryabhatt Polytechnic, Delhi (2006–2009), a Bachelor’s in Architecture from Gateway College of Architecture, Sonipat (2010–2015), and a Master’s in Urban Design from the School of Planning and Architecture, Delhi (2017–2019).
His professional work includes consulting on GIS mapping of trees at Bangalore International Airport. Research project “Third Way of Building Cities”. He has also worked with institutions like Ambedkar University Delhi on a research project on Placemaking of displacement-affected communities.
Specialisation and interest:
Urban Morphology, Participatory Urbanism, Sustainable Design, Urban Morphometrics, Space Syntax, Community Design, GIS & Cartography
