Prof. Suresh Krishna Murthy has been working as Professor and Dean with RVCA for 10yrs.,
He has a B.Arch. from M.S. Univ of Baroda and MTech from IIT Delhi in Building science & construction Management.
He has experience of 15 yrs. in academics working at Manipal Institute of Technology and RV College of Architecture and around 17 yrs. in I.T. Industry on GIS and Autodesk.
He is passionate about working in the areas of Building science & technology, and Computer Applications in Architecture.
Specialisation and interest:  Building Science & Construction Management, Climatology, environmental responsive Architecture, Spatial & Geographic information systems, Net zero and sustainability, managing the organisation's critical apps and communication systems, including email, instant messaging
