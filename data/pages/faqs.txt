FAQs ON
PROGRAMMES
What undergraduate degree programmes does RVCA currently offer?
At present, RVCA offers a single undergraduate programme: the Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch.).
What specialisations are available within the M.Arch. programme?
RVCA offers a variety of specialisations within the M.Arch. programme.  For the most current listing of available specialisations, please refer to the college website.
Does RVCA provide any online or distance learning programmes?
Currently, RVCA does not offer any online or distance learning programmes.
Can you elaborate on the curriculum for the B.Arch. programme?
The B.Arch. programme curriculum is designed to equip students with a strong foundation in design theory, technical skills, and practical experience.  A detailed breakdown of the curriculum can be found on the programme page of the RVCA website.
What internship opportunities are available to students?
RVCA leverages its strong industry connections to facilitate internship opportunities for students in well-established architectural firms.
What software programmes are utilised within the architecture programmes?
RVCA equips its students with the latest design software, including industry-standard tools like AutoCAD and Revit.
Does RVCA offer any study abroad programmes for students?
While RVCA does not have a formal study abroad programme, it may facilitate international collaborations and exchange opportunities.
What career prospects can I expect after graduating from RVCA?
Graduates from RVCA are well-prepared for careers in various fields, including architecture, urban design, construction, and related industries.
How does RVCA assist students with securing job placements?
RVCA has a dedicated placement cell that provides students with valuable resources such as resume-building assistance, interview preparation guidance, and connections to potential employers.
Can you share information about the achievements of RVCA alumni?
RVCA boasts a distinguished alumni network with successful professionals
FAQs ON
ADMISSION
What are the available admission routes for the Bachelor of Architecture (B.Arch.) programme at RVCA?
RVCA offers admission to the B.Arch. programme through three primary routes: Karnataka Common Entrance Test (KCET), Consortium of Medical, Engineering and Dental Colleges, Undergraduate Entrance Examination (COMEDK UGEEE), and Management Quota. Each route has specific eligibility criteria and a distinct selection process.
What entrance examinations are required for admission to the B.Arch. programme?
All applicants seeking admission to the B.Arch. programme must possess a valid score in either the National Aptitude Test in Architecture (NATA) or the JEE Main Paper II.
Are international applicants eligible for admission to the B.Arch. programme at RVCA?
Yes, RVCA welcomes applications from international students.  International applicants can apply through the COMEDK UGEEE or Management Quota route.
What are the minimum eligibility requirements for admission to the Master of Architecture (M.Arch.) programme?
To be eligible for admission to the M.Arch. programme, applicants must hold a Bachelor's degree in Architecture from a recognised university with a minimum of 50% marks in aggregate. Additionally, a valid score in the Postgraduate Common Entrance Test (PGCET) conducted by the Karnataka Examination Authority (KEA) or the Graduate Aptitude Test in Engineering (GATE) is mandatory.
When are the application deadlines for various admission routes?
Application deadlines for admission vary depending on the chosen route. We encourage prospective students to refer to the official RVCA website for the most up-to-date deadlines.
Does RVCA offer any scholarship programmes to students?
RVCA acknowledges the financial needs of its students and offers scholarships based on academic merit and financial hardship. Details regarding available scholarships are published on the college website.
Can prospective students schedule a campus visit before submitting their applications?
Absolutely. RVCA conducts campus tours specifically designed for students considering enrollment. Please contact the admissions office to schedule a visit.
What application documents are required for admission consideration?
The required application documents vary depending on the chosen programme and admission route. Please refer to the specific programme page on the RVCA website for detailed information.
What is the fee structure for the B.Arch. and M.Arch. programmes?
The fee structure for both the B.Arch. and M.Arch. programmes are available on the RVCA website. It is important to note that fees may vary depending on the admission quota.
How can I contact the RVCA admissions office for further inquiries?
The RVCA admissions office can be reached by phone, email, or by visiting the college website for contact details.
