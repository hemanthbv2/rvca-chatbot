Shreya Mishra
Assistant Professor
B.Arch (M.I.T.S, Gwalior), M.U.P (M.I.T.S., Gwalior)
Linkedin [http://www.linkedin.com/in/shreyavasudhamishra]
Shreya Mishra is an urban planner, architect, and academician with a Master's in Urban Planning and a Bachelor's in Architecture from MITS, Gwalior. She has served as an Assistant Professor at REVA University, Noida International University & ACA Sharda University, contributing to teaching, research, and managerial responsibility. With publications in reputed journals and expertise in sustainable urban design, Shreya is skilled in architectural pedagogy. She is passionate about fostering innovation, sustainability, and academic excellence.
Specialisation and interest: Urban Planning, Vernacular Architecture & Intellectual propert rights
