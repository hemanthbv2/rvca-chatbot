RVCA | To Build a Strong Community Around Them
Clubs
Sports Club
The sports club mainly looks after interbatch sports activities. This helps the students to know and interact with their seniors or juniors which helps them to build a strong community around them. Every year there will be interbatch sports activities which include cricket, football, basketball, carrom, table tennis, throw ball and badminton.
