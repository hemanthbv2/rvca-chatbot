Puneeth H K - rvcanew
Puneeth H K
Assistant Director
Physical Education & Sports
B.Ped, M.Ped
Mr. Puneeth H. K. holds a Master’s degree in Physical Education (M. Ped.) from University of Mysore, Mysuru (2014-15) and a Bachelor’s degree in Physical Education (B. Ped.) from University of Mysore, Mysuru (2012-13). He has more than 8 years of coaching and teaching experience in this profession. He has attended State, National and International seminars on Physical Education and been awarded certificates. He has participated in National, State and Inter-University level cricket tournaments and has been rewarded with medals and certificates. He has experience in training KCSA and club level professional cricket players. He has been nominated as a RGUHS and VTU Basketball team coach/ manager for South India Inter-University tournaments, and has also been nominated as a VTU men and women karate team coach for All India Inter-University Championship.
He  currently teaches Physical education and Social Connect and Responsibilities  at RVCA.
