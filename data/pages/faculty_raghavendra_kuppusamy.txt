Raghavendra Kuppusamy
B. Arch [SPA Vijayawada]; M. Arch. [IIT Roorkee]
Ar. Raghavendra Kuppuswamy joined RV College of Architecture in 2025. His professional career includes experiences at various South Indian firms (in Bangalore, Hyderabad, Kumbakonam etc.). His ongoing doctoral research at SASTRA University (Thanjavur) focuses on the conceptual developments in Vāstuśāstra literature. His COA-licensed work at Ekam Insights is influenced by traditional Indian architecture.
Specialisation and interest: Conceptual developments in Vāstuśāstra literature
