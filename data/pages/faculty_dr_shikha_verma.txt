Dr. Shikha Verma
Associate Professor

Linkedin [https://www.linkedin.com/in/shikha-verma-a34229200]
Dr. Shikha Verma completed her doctoral in 2010 and Master's degree in 2005 from Motial Lal Nehru National Institute of Technology, Allahabad in the area of Solid Waste Management. Besides this, she has a degree in Bachelor of Architecture from the National Institute of Technology, Bhopal (1996). She has industrial as well as teaching experience of more than 20 years. She has participated and authored in more than 30 various international and national conferences and Journals. She is a life member of various bodies which include the Council of Architecture, the Indian Geotechnical Society and the Institute of Engineers India. Her areas of interest are Sustainable Architecture with Waste Management and Geoinformatics.
Specialisation and interest: Solid Waste Management
