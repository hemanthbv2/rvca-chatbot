Sunain Dalwani is an alumnus of RVCA. She has worked at process driven architectural practices namely Cnt Architects, Samira Rathod Design Associates & DDIR Architecture Studio since her graduation. Her core learning focus is to embrace traditions while being relevant socially, environmentally & fundamentally. She actively explores allied interests in writing, research, travel & art through architecture. Her explorations have constantly informed her Architectural practice with her works involving creation of a travel research book on South Indian home architecture, inputs to redesign Bangalores’ new Dry Waste Collection centres, designing low income, accessibility friendly homes, etc apart from Residential Architecture and Interiors. Beyond architecture, she aspires to be deeply rooted in slow living practices.
