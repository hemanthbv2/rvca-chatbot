RVCA | Creative Inspiration for Aspiring Architects
Experiential Learning
ACADEMIC INDUSTRIAL INTERACTIONS
Our academic and industrial interactions programme provides practical experience, expert knowledge, skill development, and creative inspiration for aspiring architects.
Our programme equips aspiring architects with practical experience and expert knowledge. It includes internships, guest lectures, workshops, and industry visits to bridge the gap between theoretical education and real-world application. The programme prepares well-rounded architects capable of addressing the built environment's challenges and opportunities.
Let's bridge the gap between academia and industry, together!
