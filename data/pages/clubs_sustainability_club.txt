Sustainability Club
The RVCA Nature Club aspires to cultivate a community that appreciates and engages with the natural world, nurturing a sense of environmental responsibility and championing sustainable habits. Committed to enlightening both its members and the wider community, the club focuses on educating about the regional ecosystem, wildlife, and environmental concerns. Its mission extends to endorsing eco-conscious living. The club supports initiatives including recycling, minimising waste, and championing the preservation of green spaces.
