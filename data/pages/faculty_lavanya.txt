Lavanya
Lavanya Anbazhagan, an architect by profession, an artist at heart and a craftsman at hand.
After her undergrad, she was associated with Cushman and Wakefield as a Designer. She holds a master’s degree in Landscape Architecture from CEPT University, Ahmedabad. During her time at CEPT, she was involved in various activities, including  Teaching Assistantship under Prof.Snehal Kashikar, contributing to both academic and pedagogical engagement.
During her period in Ahmedabad, she also was involved in transcribing and proof reading ‘In Gratitude’, a compilation of lectures of Prof. Neelkanth Chhaya. She is also the founder of Sahakara Studio an ecological collective and a space to pay it forward for the masters who sowed the seeds of hope. An ardent artist whose hands are always occupied with the handspun yarn for crochet or the clay for pottery.
